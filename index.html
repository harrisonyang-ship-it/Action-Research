Action Research Framework & Overview

Welcome to the Action Research Studio! Action research is a disciplined, reflective inquiry conducted by educators to improve their own pedagogical practice, classroom environments, and student outcomes.

The Action Research Cycle

This studio guides you sequentially through the four core phases of reflective research:

Phase 1: Plan

Identify a problem of practice, review existing literature, and formulate actionable research questions.

Phase 2: Act

Design and implement an instructional intervention or organizational change strategy.

Phase 3: Observe

Collect triangulated quantitative and qualitative data systematically during the intervention.

Phase 4: Reflect

Analyze data, draw conclusions, evaluate impact, and plan the next instructional iteration.

How to Use This Tool

  1. Use the Question Evaluator to craft and refine your research questions in real time.
  2. Complete the Project Wizard to document your focus area, literature base, and methodology.
  3. Align your data sources using the Data Triangulation Builder.
  4. Check your draft against academic quality standards using the Self-Assessment Rubric.
  5. Export your complete Action Research Proposal to Markdown or formatted HTML for submission.
